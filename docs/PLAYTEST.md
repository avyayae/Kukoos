# Play-test kit

Your kid is the product manager. This kit is the protocol from `specs/04-roadmap.md` plus a checklist that maps what you see to where the app records it (**Grown-ups → See … progress**, `/grown-ups/progress`).

## Before
- [ ] Kukoos is installed on the tablet (`docs/INSTALL.md`) and opens from the home screen.
- [ ] Sound is on and the voice sounds good (Grown-ups → Sound & voice).
- [ ] Her profile exists and the starting point is right: carry/borrow is the focus, and add/sub within 100 without regrouping is already known.
- [ ] Daily time: goal 10 min, limit 60 min (or what you want for the test).
- [ ] Have paper and a pen ready. Don't take notes on the tablet.

## Session 1: watch silently (10 minutes)
Hand it over with one sentence: *"This is Kukoos. Show me what you can do."* Then **don't help, don't explain, don't react**. Only step in if she's stuck for more than a minute.

Note the time and the screen whenever she:

| She… | Note | Then look at |
|---|---|---|
| hesitates or looks for what to tap | screen and button | (UX fix: no data view) |
| smiles, laughs, says "yes!" | what just happened | (keep doing that) |
| gets bored, sighs, looks away | screen, minute mark | **Minutes per day** (did the session end early?) |
| gets ≥ 2 wrong in a row on the same mission | the mission | **Needs practice** and **Mistakes that keep coming back** |
| uses trade blocks or the guided column | did it help? | **Mastery by mission** (first-try % for that mission) |
| hears a hint from Kukoos | did she get it next time? | **Mistakes that keep coming back** (count per week) |
| asks to keep playing after the goal | yes / no | **Minutes per day** (total vs. lesson minutes) |

## After: two questions
1. "What was your favourite part?"
2. "What was annoying?"

Write her answers down word for word.

## Over the first week: what to track
| Signal | Where | Healthy looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Lessons per day | Summary tiles → Missions done; Minutes per day chart | Meets the 10-minute goal most days |
| Voluntary returns | Minutes per day chart (days with minutes she started herself) | Several days without being asked |
| Frustration moments (≥ 2 wrong in a row on the same skill) | Needs practice; first-try accuracy per day | Accuracy dips get better within a few days |
| Misconceptions (e.g. *Smaller from larger*, *forgot to carry*) | Mistakes that keep coming back (7- and 14-day counts) | Counts fall week over week |
| Progress | Mastery by mission (👑 = crowned) | New crowns every few days |

## Turning notes into fixes
- **≥ 2 wrong in a row on the same skill, often:** tune the difficulty. Move the starting point back one mission, or note the skill for a generator change.
- **The same misconception all week:** check the Kukoos hint text for that mistake and whether trade blocks show the trade clearly.
- **Bored before the goal:** shorter missions or more celebration. Note which screen.
- **Hesitation on a screen:** a UX issue. Note the screen and what she tried to tap.

Keep the notes with the date. They feed the next feature spec.
